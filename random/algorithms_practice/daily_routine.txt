The 1-1-1 Daily Routine
Every time you sit down to train, split your session into three quick parts:

Warm-up (5–10 mins) — Same Problem: Re-code one problem you solved yesterday completely from scratch without looking at past code. This verifies that you retained the logic, syntax, and edge cases.

Review (10–15 mins) — Variation Problem: Solve a new, slightly different problem using a pattern you learned a few days ago. This tests whether you actually recognized the pattern or just memorized one specific question.

New Mastery (20–30 mins) — New Pattern: Learn and build your first solution for a brand new pattern
